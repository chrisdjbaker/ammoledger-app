Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the AmmoLedger app for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Android (“the app”).

We don’t collect your data

The developer does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no servers.

What stays on your device

Your ammo types, containers, counts, locations, lots, purchases, history, range log and any photos you add are stored on your device. The optional name, address and policy number for the insurance report are also stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Optional iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, a copy of your inventory is kept in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it stays the same on your iPhone and iPad. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The developer cannot see it. Photos are not synced.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends your inventory to your watch, and changes you make on the watch are sent back to your iPhone. This happens directly between your own devices.

Camera, photos and Face ID

The camera is used only to scan barcodes. Photos you choose for the insurance report are saved inside the app. If you turn on the Face ID lock, Face ID is handled by iOS; the app only learns whether unlocking succeeded.

Files you share

Reports, CSV files and backups are only created when you ask for them, and only go where you send them.

Android

The Android version works the same way, without iCloud Sync, the Apple Watch app or widgets. Your inventory, photos and history are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include this data in your own Google account backup, which the developer can’t see. Scanning uses Google’s code scanner, part of Google Play services. The camera picture is read on your phone, and Google Play services may send Google basic diagnostic information (such as the device model and app version) under Google’s privacy policy. The developer receives none of it. The optional app lock uses your phone’s own fingerprint, face or screen lock; the app never sees them. Low-stock reminders are notifications made on your phone. The app has no internet access.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
